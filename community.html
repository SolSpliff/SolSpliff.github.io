Get Involved with SolSpliff

We’re more than just a token — we’re a community-driven movement. Here’s how you can get involved and make your voice heard:

1. Join Our Social Media Platforms

Stay up-to-date with the latest SolSpliff news and events by joining our official community channels:

  • Telegram – Get instant updates and chat with the team.
  • Twitter – Follow us for updates, memes, and more.

2. Vote on Key Decisions

SolSpliff is community-governed. You’ll have the power to vote on key issues like:

  • Which charities to support.
  • How creator fees should be spent (marketing, development, etc.).
  • Potential partnerships and promotional efforts.

Voting access is verified through our community bot, which links your Telegram ID with your wallet. To protect your privacy, the bot saves only minimal verification data and stores it in encrypted form. Raw wallet signatures are never kept — only encrypted verification results and timestamps are retained.

3. Participate in Contests & Events

We love engaging with our community! From meme contests to exclusive giveaways, there’s always something fun happening. Keep an eye on our social media for announcements.

4. Become a SolSpliff Ambassador

Want to help spread the word? You can become an ambassador and promote SolSpliff on social media, at events, and more. Reach out to us if you're interested in becoming a part of the ambassador program!

DAO Governance & Owner Agreement

The DAO can propose and vote on governance decisions. However, changes that would modify the owner's 12% share of Pump.fun creator rewards require both a successful DAO vote and explicit agreement from the project owners. The owner's 12% share cannot be changed by the DAO alone.
