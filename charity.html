SolSpliff Charity Initiatives

Giving Back to the Community

At SolSpliff, we believe in using the power of cryptocurrency for good. A portion of the creator fees from every SolSpliff transaction is allocated to our Charity Fund. What makes SolSpliff unique is that the community votes on which causes receive donations.

How It Works

Each time SolSpliff is bought, sold, or transferred, a portion of the transaction fees goes into the Charity Fund. The community votes on which charities or causes should receive donations, and we distribute funds based on the winning vote.

  • 1. Community Voting: SolSpliff holders vote to choose the recipient charities or initiatives.
  • 2. Charitable Donations: After a vote concludes, a percentage of accumulated creator fees is donated to the winning cause(s).
  • 3. Transparency: We publish donation receipts, transaction evidence, and summaries where possible so the community can see how funds were used.

Founder / Project History

The individual(s) behind SolSpliff have previously fundraised personally for charities, including:

  • Canadian Cancer Society — personal fundraising and volunteer efforts to support cancer research and patient services.
  • Heels to Heal — fundraising for a women's shelter through volunteer and donation-driven events.
  • Terry Fox Run — fundraising for cancer research.

Important: SolSpliff as a project is new and has not yet made any project-level donations. The founder’s past fundraising work was done personally and separate from the SolSpliff project.

Current & Future Campaigns

Because SolSpliff is newly launched, official project donations will begin once the Charity Fund accumulates sufficient funds and the community votes on recipients. Planned steps before the first project donation:

  • Open a community vote to select the first recipient charity.
  • Accumulate and earmark creator-fee funds for donation.
  • Publish donation details (recipient, amount, and supporting transaction evidence) after funds are sent.

How to Propose a Charity

If you want to nominate a charity or local cause for consideration, please share the organization’s name, a short description, and any supporting links in our community channels. Nominations will be collected prior to each voting round.

Transparency & Proof of Donation

We are committed to being transparent. For each donation we aim to publish:

  • Recipient organization name and contact (as provided by the charity).
  • Donation amount and date.
  • On-chain transaction hash or receipt where available.
  • A short summary of the intended use (if provided by the recipient).

Note: in some cases third-party charities may not provide immediate on-chain receipts; when that occurs we will publish alternative proof such as official acknowledgement, receipts and or email confirmations.

Eligibility & Limitations

  • Donations are funded from a portion of creator fees; amounts depend on trading activity and collected fees.
  • We will not route Charity Fund money for operational expenses; funds earmarked for charity will be reserved for donation unless the community votes otherwise.
  • Because SolSpliff is a new project, initial donations are subject to the Charity Fund balance and completed community votes.

Contact & Questions

If you have questions about charity nominations, transparency, or past fundraising, contact us at solsplf@gmail.com or post in the community channels. We welcome suggestions and will explain the nomination and voting timeline before each round.
