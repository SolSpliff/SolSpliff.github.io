SolSpliff Roadmap

Our Vision for the Future

SolSpliff ({{TICKER}}) is more than just a token; it’s a movement. Here’s our roadmap outlining the key milestones that will guide us based on market cap growth and community progress.

$0 – $100K Market Cap: Foundation Phase

  • Website and socials launch
  • {{TICKER}} goes live on Pump.fun
  • Organic community growth and meme expansion
  • Partnerships with early supporters
  • Initial charity & awareness initiatives

$100K – $500K Market Cap: Growth Phase

  • First listings on external DEX/CEX platforms
  • Marketing pushes, collaborations, and viral campaigns
  • Community events and holder rewards

$500K – $2M Market Cap: Expansion Phase

  • Development of NFT or art tie-ins
  • Utility Talks Start
  • Strategic brand & ecosystem partnerships
  • Community treasury foundation (pre-DAO structure)
  • Liquidity strengthening and scaling initiatives

$2M – $10M Market Cap: Legacy Phase

  • DAO framework discussions begin (no activation yet)
  • Third-party audits & transparency reports
  • Integration with larger Solana ecosystem tools
  • Charitable allocations scaled with trading volume

$10M+ Market Cap: DAO Activation

  • DAO officially launched with governance voting
  • Community proposals and treasury decisions
  • Long-term sustainability and ecosystem growth
