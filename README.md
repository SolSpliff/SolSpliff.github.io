## 🪙 Project Overview

This project is launching on **[Pump.Fun](https://pump.fun)** — a decentralized launchpad built on **Solana**.

Because the token launches via Pump.Fun:
- The **creator does not control the contract**.
- There are **no presales** or **manual token allocations**.
- **All tokens are public** and tradable at launch.
- The creator **cannot modify** or withdraw liquidity.

The only benefit to the creator is a **small percentage of creator rewards** automatically distributed by Pump.Fun when liquidity is created.  
This ensures a fair, transparent, and permissionless launch process.

---

## 💰 Creator Fee Allocations

The creator rewards (from Pump.Fun’s 1% creator fee) are distributed and governed transparently under the DAO framework:

| Allocation | Percentage | Description |
|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| **Promotions & Advertising** | 30% | Marketing, community growth, paid promos, influencer collaborations. |
| **Development & Bots** | 25% | Ongoing development (website, bots, tooling) and operational costs. |
| **Charity / Community Fund** | 18% | Donations and community grants determined by DAO votes. |
| **Team & Operational Expenses** | 12% | Non-speculative operating costs & contractual obligations. |
| **Locking Rewards & Incentives** | 10% | Short-term staking/locking incentives and reward pools. |
| **Buybacks & Burns** | 5% | Market buybacks and controlled burns to support tokenomics. |
| **Total** | **100%** |  |

These allocations apply **only** to the creator rewards distributed by Pump.Fun — not the token supply itself.

---

## 🔒 Governance & DAO Control

The DAO governs fund usage and allocations through community voting.  
However, to ensure operational fairness and sustainability:

> ⚠️ **The Team’s 12% allocation of creator rewards cannot be changed or reassigned by DAO vote unless the Team (owners) also agree.**

This safeguard protects the project from hostile or reckless governance decisions that could threaten continuity.

All other allocations can be adjusted or reallocated through verified DAO voting proposals.
